About e-Seva Portal

e-Seva Portal is a citizen-centric digital platform designed to bring multiple government services together in one convenient place.

Citizens can apply for government certificates and services, upload required documents, track application progress and register complaints without visiting multiple offices.

The platform focuses on transparency, accessibility, faster service delivery and better communication between citizens and government departments.
